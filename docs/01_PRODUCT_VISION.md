# Product Vision

## Promise

**EduBridge — one clear bridge from school to home.** A teacher creates a structured update for a student; the linked parent receives and acknowledges it; the principal can see whether important communication is reaching families.

## Vision and differentiator

EduBridge is not another school ERP or noisy group chat. Its single **School-to-Home Communication Loop** is:

1. Teacher sends a structured student update.
2. Linked parent receives it live.
3. Parent acknowledges it.
4. Teacher sees acknowledgement status.
5. Principal sees own-school coverage derived from the same records.

The product creates clarity and verification without surveillance, social feeds, or AI. The student is the protected subject of the record, not a V1 system operator.

## Principles

- Teacher input takes under one minute.
- Parent understands an update and any required action in five seconds.
- One authorised source of truth serves teacher, parent, and principal views.
- Acknowledgement means seen, never completed/learned/agreed.
- The design is calm, mobile-ready, accessible, and honest about data.
