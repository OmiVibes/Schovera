# Student Knowledge Guide

## Explain EduBridge simply

“EduBridge helps students stay connected to learning. A teacher posts one clear update about what happened in class and what to do next. The student and parent see it in one place and can mark that they have seen it. The teacher can then know who may still need a reminder.”

## Inputs, process, outputs

| Part | Simple explanation |
|---|---|
| Input | The teacher chooses an assigned class/student and types a category, title, and message. A linked parent can press ‘seen’. |
| Process | The website checks who is signed in and their stored school role/link. It saves the update and makes it available only to the linked parent and authorised school users. |
| Output | Parent sees a clear child update; teacher sees acknowledgement status; principal sees school-level coverage. |

## Technology explanation

- **Website:** the pages people use in a browser.
- **Secure sign-in:** checks if someone is a teacher, student, or parent.
- **Database:** the organised digital record book that saves updates and acknowledgements.
- **Permissions:** rules that stop people seeing information for another class or child.
- **Responsive design:** the same website rearranges so it is usable on phones and computers.

## What it is not

It does not mark attendance, calculate marks, replace teachers, read private chats, or use artificial intelligence. It does not know whether homework was completed; “seen” only means someone saw the update.

## Honest limitations

- It needs a device/internet connection to use live updates.
- It depends on teachers writing short, clear updates.
- It has not yet been proven across many schools.
- Acknowledgement cannot prove learning.

## Why create it?

“I noticed that students can fall behind when information is only verbal, in a notebook, or hidden in group messages. I wanted one simple place that connects the classroom, the student, and the parent.”

Do not memorise jargon. Practise explaining the actual demo with the words above, then answer slowly and honestly if a feature is future work.
