# Business Model

## Value proposition

EduBridge gives schools a focused way to prevent avoidable learning gaps caused by scattered communication. Teachers save repeat explanations; parents get a trusted, child-specific view; students know their next step.

## Customer, user, and payer

- **Customer/payer:** school principal, coordinator, or school management.
- **Daily users:** teachers, students, parents.
- **Initial buyer:** a small private or independent school willing to run a class-level pilot.

## Sensible commercial path

1. Free time-limited pilot for one class or a small school cohort, with setup support.
2. Paid annual subscription per school after the pilot proves teacher usage and family engagement.
3. Price by active school size only after interviews, local purchasing research, and pilot support costs are understood.

Do not quote invented revenue, market size, or pricing figures at Robotex. Say the model is a school subscription because the school gains operational value and should not make students pay for basic access.

## Costs to acknowledge

Hosting/database/authentication, domain, teacher onboarding, support, safeguarding/privacy work, and future translations/mobile notifications. A simple stack keeps early costs low.

## Alternatives and positioning

| Alternative | Gap EduBridge addresses |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp/class groups | Fast but unstructured; messages disappear; no child-specific acknowledgement. |
| Paper diary/notebook | Depends on a student carrying/writing it correctly; unavailable during absence. |
| Full school ERP | Broad, expensive, and often difficult to adopt for a single communication problem. |
| Generic LMS | Often content/course-centred rather than a quick daily class-to-home continuity loop. |

## Metrics for a pilot

- Median teacher time to publish an update.
- Percentage of published updates acknowledged within 24/48 hours.
- Number of repeated “what did we miss?” queries reported by teachers.
- Weekly active teachers and families.
- Short satisfaction interviews; not vanity downloads.

## Ethical boundary

Acknowledgement is a communication signal, not a measure of parenting, student effort, or learning quality. Schools must not use it to punish families without context.
