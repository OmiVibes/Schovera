# Preliminary competitor comparison

Checked against public vendor documentation on 17 November 2026. These are vendor-described capabilities, **not independent hands-on tests, endorsement, price quotes or a complete market survey**. Product packaging can change. “Not verified here” means no conclusion; it does not mean a competitor lacks the feature.

| Product | Verified public description | Fair implication for Schovera |
|---|---|---|
| ClassDojo (international) | Its help page documents translating teacher messages/story posts and returning to the original; it separately notes limitations for event-post translation. [Official help](https://help.classdojo.com/hc/en-us/articles/37573538259085-Translate-Messages-and-Story-Posts) | Multilingual parent communication is established, not a novel invention. Compare the exact school workflow, not an unsupported language-count claim. |
| ParentSquare (international) | Its FAQ describes school communication, attendance, multiple delivery channels and automatic two-way translation. [Official FAQ](https://www.parentsquare.com/resources/faqs/) | Schovera's current local prototype cannot claim equivalent hosted, SMS/voice or two-way family conversation coverage. |
| MyClassboard (India) | Its Parent App page lists attendance summaries, circulars, homework, timetables and communication tools. [Official Parent App](https://www.myclassboard.com/mobile-apps/parent-app/) | Attendance plus communication is already available in Indian school software. Schovera must demonstrate a useful, affordable workflow rather than claiming to be first. |

For these vendors, this review did not independently verify current package prices, exact paid tiers, country availability, outage durability, signed-event contracts, teacher-finalization semantics, translation review permissions, data retention or setup effort. No comparative “No” boxes are justified for those untested items.

For **Schovera**, local synthetic tests demonstrate role-scoped communication/acknowledgement, original preservation with optional reviewed translation, explicit classroom finalization/correction history, a signed mock-reader/SQLite path and safe held-work review. Its public deployment, physical reader/Pi driver, real SMS/calls, commercial support and customer acceptance remain unimplemented or unverified. See [feature status](FEATURE_STATUS.md).

Proposed differentiation: a focused school workflow that clearly separates original messages, reviewed translations, gate evidence, Teacher confirmation and retained offline intentions. This is a **product-positioning proposal**, not proof of unique intellectual property or a feature absent from every competitor.

On “Why not WhatsApp?”: a familiar messaging tool may suit many schools. Schovera proposes linked-child access, class authorization, classroom register revisions and evidence review in one school workflow. Actual school preference and migration cost require research. This comparison did not audit WhatsApp's current product features or privacy terms, so do not claim it has no security, groups, read receipts or language support.
